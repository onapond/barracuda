Status: Revised

Scope
- `/brand`
- `/coffee`

Rule
- this sheet defines route jobs and hierarchy only
- it does not write final copy

Corrected concept axis
- `우리들만의 프라이빗 공간`

---

## Route: `/brand`

Page objective
- explain why Barracuda is not an ordinary cafe by making its private-space identity and distinctive cafe character clear from the start

5-second understanding target
- Barracuda is a special cafe that can also become our private space

Primary message
- Barracuda is a cafe, but it is built for people who want to gather in a more private and distinctive way

Secondary support message
- coffee, interior, and table distance together make the space feel different from a routine cafe visit

Keep
- strong visual hero
- two gallery sections
- short closing brand note

Compress
- abstract atmosphere language
- generic brand self-description

Remove
- any line that explains the page’s editorial structure
- any line that hides the private-space proposition behind mood-language

Proof block decision
- show proof through private gatherings, table distance, calmness, and differentiated cafe use

CTA decision
- no hard sales CTA required
- route still stays identity-led, but the proposition must be explicit enough without sales language

Must not try to communicate
- a vague design-brand mood board
- a full founder story
- generic lifestyle branding

---

## Route: `/coffee`

Page objective
- show how Barracuda coffee supports the experience of a special cafe, not just a coffee process story

5-second understanding target
- coffee is part of why Barracuda feels like a special cafe, not just another place to order a drink

Primary message
- Barracuda coffee makes the space feel more distinct, more considered, and more worth staying in

Secondary support message
- the menu and coffee experience support the private-space feeling instead of disrupting it

Keep
- hero
- process gallery
- menu-related section
- full menu showcase

Compress
- roastery-process boasting
- abstract continuity language

Remove
- lines that explain page composition rather than coffee character
- any route copy that could belong to any premium roastery site

Proof block decision
- keep proof in how coffee, menu, and cafe experience feel more distinct than an ordinary stop-in cafe
- do not invent technical coffee claims not already supported by the route

CTA decision
- primary CTA stays menu-facing
- secondary movement can point toward store later, but not in this copy phase

Must not try to communicate
- technical roast authority as the main identity
- specialty coffee jargon for its own sake
- generic premium cafe copy

---

## Batch 1 IA Conclusion

Shared route rule
- both routes must have one dominant job each
- `/brand` defines why Barracuda is a special cafe and private space
- `/coffee` supports why that cafe experience feels special

Orchestrator note
- the rewrite must get more legible, earlier, on privacy and distinctiveness
